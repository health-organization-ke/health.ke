Why Embu Level 5 Hospital Needs You

Every day, hundreds of patients walk through our doors — mothers, fathers, children, and elders — all trusting us with their health and dignity. Behind every recovery, every moment of comfort, and every life saved, there is a nurse or caregiver.

Right now, the demand for compassionate care is growing faster than the number of hands available to provide it. Our wards are busy. Our emergency department never sleeps. And many of our experienced staff are stretched thin.

We are not just looking for people to fill positions.
We are looking for people who understand that nursing and caregiving is more than a job — it is a calling.

When you join Embu Level 5 Hospital, you become part of a team that stands between illness and recovery, between fear and hope. Your presence can mean the difference between a patient feeling alone and feeling cared for.

We need nurses who bring skill and calm under pressure.
We need caregivers who bring patience and kindness.

If that sounds like you — we are ready for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions from applicants.

Who can apply for these positions? +
We welcome applications from qualified nurses and caregivers. Both experienced professionals and those early in their careers are encouraged to apply.
Is a professional license required? +
For nursing positions, a valid license is preferred but not mandatory at the application stage. Caregiver roles do not require a professional license. The field is optional.
How long does the recruitment process take? +
After submitting your application, our team reviews it and contacts shortlisted candidates within 2–3 weeks for the next steps.
Where is Embu Level 5 Hospital located? +
The hospital is located in Embu Town along the Embu–Nairobi / Meru Highway. You can see the exact location on the map above.
